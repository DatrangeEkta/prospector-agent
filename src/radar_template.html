Every month the Prospector reads new arXiv research in quantitative finance and in LLMs and AI, ranks it against what our teams work on, and explains the best papers in plain words. Each quarter we pilot one technique and measure what it changes.

How to use this page (2 minutes)

  1. 1
    Scroll to "This month's shortlist". There are 10 cards, one idea each.
  2. 2
    Read the "Use for us" line. Look for an idea that sounds like your own work. You don't need to read the paper.
  3. 3
    Tap "I'd pilot this" on any idea that would help you. The most popular idea becomes this quarter's pilot.
Example

Ask the agent

Ask any question about your work. The agent searches our papers, the local AI checks if they really answer it, and if not it decides where to look next (older papers, or a live arXiv search with its own words) until it has enough. Every step appears live as the agent takes it. It first checks your question, and at the end it checks every claim against the paper's own abstract and gives a confidence. Nothing is saved to the settings.

Tell Prospector

Type your interests or what you worked on today, in plain words. Example: "My interest is invoice matching. Today I got stuck on OCR breaking merged cells in fee tables." The next scan uses it.

Sources
arXiv, free API. Quant: q-fin portfolio, risk, statistics, computational, trading, general. LLM / AI: cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, narrowed to LLM papers on our topics.

      
How papers are ranked
By code, never by the model: weighted keywords in the title and abstract plus closeness in meaning to what our teams do (a local embedding model), with newer papers scoring higher. Each area gets its own top five, so the larger AI feed cannot crowd out quant work.

      
Who decides
The local model only writes the short notes. People read the papers, vote, and choose the quarter's pilot. Papers already shown in an earlier scan are skipped.
